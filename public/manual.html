Adding Content & Shows

Two primary discovery pathways allow you to curate and populate your active rotation without clutter: search millions of titles in the global catalog, or leverage your Binge Buddies network through two frictionless friend-vetted borrowing methods.

Two Core Pathways & Three Direct Entry Points

CouchTaterz is designed for rapid, friction-free show discovery. You can curate your queue across two primary discovery pathways (featuring three direct entry points):
  • 1. Pathway 1: Global Search ("+ Add Shows" Modal): Open the global catalog modal from the dashboard header or bottom nav. Search millions of titles with instant auto-suggestions, Rotten Tomatoes scores (e.g., 🍅 98%), and platform badges (Apple TV+, Hulu, Max, Netflix).
  • 2A. Pathway 2A: Social Discovery via "Buddy's Picks" Tab: Inside the Add Shows modal, switch to the Buddy's Picks tab. Browse trusted titles recommended and rated by your inner circle (like Julio, AnnaDee, and Rafael) and add them to your queue with a single click.
  • 2B. Pathway 2B: Social Discovery via Friends' Dashboards: Switch directly to any connected friend's dashboard using the Binge Buddies dropdown in the top header. As you explore what your buddies are watching or have queued up, click the "+" button directly on any show card to instantly copy it into your own queue.
Queue Targeting: When adding any title, choose which queue it lands in (Watching, Up Next, or Watched). For an in-depth breakdown of tier filters, episode trackers, and air banner states, see Section 02 below.

The Three Queue Tiers

  • Watching: Active priorities. Gives your AI assistant clear boundary limits to prevent spoilers and push live release alerts.
  • Up Next: Your organized backlog. Automatically stages shows until space clears in your active rotation.
  • Watched: Completed series history for long-term ratings, rewatch tracking, and friend recommendations.
